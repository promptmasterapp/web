How it works

Short lessons. Real practice. Clear feedback.

Every lesson takes about five minutes and ends with you writing prompts, not just reading about them.

  1. 1

    Pick your role

    Architect, developer, teacher, marketer, student or simply curious. Your lessons and examples follow what you actually do.

  2. 2

    Practise five ways

    Compare, fill in the blanks, put in order, spot the flaw and write your own. Each type trains a different skill.

  3. 3

    Get scored, get better

    The prompts you write are scored on context, clarity, format and constraints — and you see exactly what to add.

Role-based paths

Lessons built around your work

Each role gets a ten-unit path that runs from the fundamentals through clarity and advanced techniques to mastery. You can switch fields at any time.

  • Architect

    Image generation, presentations

    Describe a space, a style and a light well enough that an image tool gets it right.

  • Developer

    Code, debugging

    Practise prompts for debugging and code review, with the context an assistant needs.

Frequently asked questions

Is PromptMaster free?

Yes. You can use every lesson for free. The free version shows ads between lessons and limits your hearts; PromptMaster Premium removes ads and gives you unlimited hearts, unlimited review rounds and automatic streak protection.

Do I need an account?

No. There is no sign-up at all. Your progress is saved on your device only.

Does PromptMaster generate AI answers?

No. PromptMaster doesn’t connect to an AI tool and doesn’t produce AI output. It teaches you prompt-writing techniques that you can use in the AI tools you already use.

Who is it for?

Anyone who wants better results from AI — from students to grandparents. Large touch targets, readable type and plain language make it easy to use at any age.

Are the league rivals real people?

No. The weekly league opponents are fictional players generated on your device. Nobody else can see you, and no data is shared.

How do I cancel Premium?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google. Cancel in your App Store or Google Play account settings at least 24 hours before the current period ends. Step-by-step instructions are on our Support page.
